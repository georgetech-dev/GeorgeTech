About GeorgeTech

Business understanding first. Technology second.

GeorgeTech is being developed as an independent UK business technology studio focused on useful, understandable systems for small organisations.

The idea behind GeorgeTech

Build around the real job.

Many small businesses do not need a huge platform. They need a website that explains the service, a booking journey that makes sense, or one reliable place to see customers, jobs, documents and next actions.

GeorgeTech starts by understanding the work as it happens now. The technology follows from that—not the other way around.

Practical experienceA background in trades, property services and day-to-day operations informs the design.
Business developmentOngoing Business and Management study supports structured planning and decision-making.
Hands-on buildingWebsites, integrations, dashboards and connected systems are developed and tested directly.
Continuous learningTraining focuses on security, responsible data handling and stronger technical delivery.
